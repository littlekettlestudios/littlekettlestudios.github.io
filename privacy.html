Privacy policy

Sprout Sort · effective 27 October 2026

Sprout Sort is a game made by Little Kettle Studios LLC, an Oregon limited liability company (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Sprout Sort app and to this website.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and ad information to show, measure and protect those ads. If you decline tracking, the game asks for non-personalised ads.
  • We get gameplay statistics through Amplitude to balance and fix the game. They're tied to a random code for your install of the game, never to your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time. See Your choices.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Sprout Sort is free and is paid for by ads. Ads are always optional reward videos you choose to watch (for extra moves, double coins or a power-up). From stage 30 there are also occasional ads between levels, which the Ad-Free purchase removes. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and the ad content is limited to Google's “G” (general audiences) rating.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the game collects:

  • your device's IP address, which Google uses to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city, never your precise location);
  • device and app information, such as device model, iOS version, language, and network type;
  • identifiers: Google's own identifiers for this app, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • advertising data, such as which ads you saw or tapped, and how you interacted with them;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ad software, such as crash logs and load times.

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, count views and taps, prevent fraud and abuse, and to personalise ads. Your advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking, and if you decline (or, in the EEA and UK, don't consent), the game asks Google for non-personalised ads. Google is responsible for this information under the Google Privacy Policy. Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law requires consent, Google's consent message lists the ad partners and the purposes in detail.

We don't receive any of this information about you. AdMob gives us only totals, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned.

Your choices

  • Tracking (all players). From your first result at stage 30, iOS asks whether Sprout Sort may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the game asks Google for non-personalised ads, and every feature still works. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent and opt-out (where the law provides it). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are personalised. In US states with privacy laws, a message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for targeted ads. You can review or change these choices at any time in Sprout Sort under Settings → Privacy choices for ads. That button appears wherever one of these messages applies.
  • Ad-Free. The Ad-Free purchase removes the ads between levels. Reward videos stay available if you want them, and you only see one when you choose to.
  • Statistics. The gameplay statistics below are tied only to a random code for this install, not to who you are. A new install starts a new code. To stop new statistics being sent, you can delete the game.

Gameplay statistics (Amplitude)

The game sends short statistics to Amplitude, an analytics service run by Amplitude, Inc. in the United States, which processes them for us. They describe play, not people. Examples:

  • which stage was played, whether it was won, how many moves were left, and which power-ups or reward ads were used;
  • shop and event activity, such as an item bought with coins or a Weekend Sprint score;
  • that Ad-Free was bought, or that a purchase was cancelled or failed (not by whom, and no payment details);
  • whether you allowed tracking and whether an ad consent message was answered (only the choice, nothing about you);
  • how many crashes or freezes happened, and what kind of save or loading problem occurred;
  • basics that come with every statistic: app version, iOS version, device model, language, your country, and when a play session starts and ends.

They contain no name, email, contact details, Apple Account, advertising identifier or precise location. Each install is represented by a random code the game makes the first time it runs, which isn't connected to your device's other identifiers. The game tells Amplitude not to store your IP address, city or mobile carrier; Amplitude works out your country from the connection and keeps only that. We use these statistics only to balance difficulty, fix problems and decide what to improve. We never sell them, share them with advertisers or use them for ads.

Game settings from our website

The game may download a small settings file from littlekettlestudios.com, so we can adjust things like event dates and ad pacing without an update. The request sends nothing about you, but like any web request it reaches our website host, GitHub, which may log your IP address for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive those logs.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or freezes, iOS gives the game a report the next time it opens (Apple's MetricKit). The report stays on your iPhone. Only the number of crashes and freezes is included in the gameplay statistics above. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in the Settings app (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may also share crash reports with us under Apple's privacy policy.

Your progress, iCloud and Game Center

Your progress is saved on your iPhone: stages, coins, petals, your shop and its name, your shopkeeper and your settings. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account so it comes back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone. Apple stores that copy under Apple's privacy policy. It isn't sent to us, and we can't see it.

If you sign in to Game Center, your Weekend Sprint score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see it with your Game Center name. The game reads the leaderboard to show ranks but doesn't send your Game Center details to us. You can play every part of the game without signing in.

Purchases

The Ad-Free purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The game only learns from Apple whether Ad-Free is owned, so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase on your other devices.

What we don't do

No accounts or sign-ups. No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. No chat or sharing between players. We don't sell your personal information for money. Personalised ads through Google may count as “selling” or “sharing” under some US state laws, and you can opt out of that (see Your choices).

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

For the gameplay statistics, Little Kettle Studios LLC is the controller, and Amplitude processes them on our behalf. Google is an independent controller for the information it collects through AdMob. Our legal bases are:

  • Consent for personalised ads, and for storing or reading information on your device for ads, collected through Google's consent message. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • Legitimate interests for gameplay statistics and crash counts (keeping the game working and fair to play), and for the limited ad processing that Google's consent message describes under legitimate interests, which you can object to in that message.
  • Contract for providing the game and restoring a purchase you made.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because our statistics are filed only under a random install code, we usually can't tell which ones are yours, so we may not be able to find any data to give you. Tell us what you need and we'll help, and we'll pass on requests about ads to Google where we can. You can also contact Google directly, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Google and Amplitude may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Both rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

If you're in the United States

Residents of California, Oregon, Colorado, Virginia, and other states with privacy laws have rights over their personal information. In the last 12 months, the game collected these categories, through Google AdMob, for advertising:

  • identifiers (Google's app identifiers, and the advertising identifier if you allowed tracking);
  • internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions and app usage);
  • approximate location from your IP address.

Through Amplitude, for analytics only, it also collected a random install identifier, game activity (internet or other electronic network activity) and your country. That information isn't sold or shared for advertising.

When ads are personalised, this may count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out in Sprout Sort under Settings → Privacy choices for ads, or by choosing Ask App Not to Track in the iPhone Settings app. We don't collect sensitive personal information, and we don't knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16.

You may also ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information. Email us from any address, or have an authorised agent do so. We'll answer within 45 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our answer with “Appeal” in the subject. If you're unhappy with the outcome, you can contact your state attorney general.

Children

Sprout Sort is a general-audience game. It's rated 4+ because its content suits all ages, but it isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where they live). Ads in the game are limited to general-audience content. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

How long information is kept

  • Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud until you delete it. Deleting the game removes the copy on your iPhone. The iCloud copy can be removed in the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, when Sprout Sort is listed there.
  • Gameplay statistics are kept in Amplitude for as long as its service stores them for our account. We use them only in summary form.
  • Google keeps advertising information according to the Google retention policy.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need them to help you, and deleted within two years of the last message.

Security

Information sent by the game travels over encrypted connections. Because we keep nothing that names you, only a random install code, there's very little that could be exposed.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects how your information is used in a significant way, we'll say so in the game's update notes.
