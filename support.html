Help and feedback

Sprout Sort

Sending feedback from the beta

Take a screenshot while playing, tap Share Beta Feedback, and add a note. You can also open the TestFlight app, choose Sprout Sort, and tap Send Beta Feedback. If the game crashes, TestFlight will offer to send a report: please say yes.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone, after every stage. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your iCloud account, so reinstalling the game or moving to a new iPhone brings it back.

Purchases and ads in the beta

They're simulated. Nothing is ever charged, and the "test ad" screens are stand-ins drawn by the game.

Weekend Sprint ranks

Ranks need Game Center. Sign in from the Settings app on your iPhone to appear on the weekly leaderboard; everything else works without it.
