Help and feedback

Sprout Sort

Contact us

Email contact@littlekettlestudios.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the game's version (in Sprout Sort, open Settings and look under About), your stage number, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

How do I play?

Tap a pot, then tap a shelf with a free spot to move it there (or drag it). Line up three of the same plant on one shelf and they bloom and clear. Clear every pot within the stage's moves, shown on the watering can. You get three free undos on each attempt. New ideas arrive as you go, such as pots hidden at the back, split and wide shelves, locks and mystery pots, and a short card explains each one the first time it appears.

I ran out of moves, or my bench is jammed

When the moves run out, you can add +5 moves with coins or by watching a reward video, and carry on with the same board. If every spot is full and nothing can bloom, Continue adds a spare shelf. Or tap Try again: there are no lives, so you can retry as often as you like. Power-ups (Shuffle, Spare Shelf and Harvest) unlock as you clear stages 10, 20 and 35.

Is my progress saved? What about a new iPhone?

Yes. Progress is saved on your iPhone as you play. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your iCloud account too. Install the game on your new iPhone with the same Apple Account and your progress comes back. Without iCloud, deleting the game deletes your progress.

How do I restore Ad-Free?

Open Sprout Sort, tap Settings, then Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. Ad-Free also turns on by itself when the game starts on a device signed in to that account.

What does Ad-Free remove?

The ads that appear between levels, from stage 30 onward. Reward videos (+5 moves, double coins, a free power-up) are always your choice, so they stay available after Ad-Free. You only see one when you tap for it.

A purchase went wrong, or I'd like a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Sprout Sort. If you were charged but Ad-Free didn't turn on, try Restore purchases first, then email us.

A reward video won't load

Reward videos need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available for a moment. Wait a minute and try again. In the EEA and the UK, ads load only after you've answered the consent message.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

To change tracking, open the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where a consent or opt-out message applies (for example in the EEA, the UK, or US states with privacy laws), open Sprout Sort → Settings → Privacy choices for ads. The privacy policy has the details.

Weekend Sprint ranks and Game Center

Events open once you've cleared stage 12. The Weekend Sprint runs Friday to Sunday: clear as many stages as you can in 5 minutes on the clock, and your best run counts. Ranks need Game Center. Sign in from the iPhone Settings app → Game Center to appear on the weekly leaderboard and earn rank petals. Everything else works without it.

Can I delete my data?

Your progress lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Deleting the game removes it from your iPhone. The iCloud copy can be removed in the iPhone Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, when Sprout Sort is listed there. We don't hold anything that identifies you, but if you have a privacy request, email us with “Privacy” in the subject.
